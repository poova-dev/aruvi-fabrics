Artisan Roots

Woven with Integrity in Pattukkottai

At Aruvi Fabrics, we believe that true luxury lies in authentic natural fibers, skilled handloom craftsmanship, and designs that honor the everyday grace of Indian women.

Our Philosophy

Fabrics that breathe, drape, and endure.

Pattukkottai, located in the fertile Cauvery Delta region of Tamil Nadu, has long been surrounded by rich cultural traditions of craftsmanship. Aruvi Fabrics was born to bring the authentic touch of pure combed cotton, handloom weaves, and celebratory soft silks directly to discerning customers.

We reject fast, disposable polyester in favor of skin-friendly weaves that soften gracefully with every wash. Whether it is our staple Cotton Short Tops or our celebratory Soft Silk ensembles, each piece represents genuine textile dignity.
