How to Measure

Four Key Measurements

1

Bust

Measure horizontally around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the measuring tape comfortably snug but not tight.

2

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, generally an inch above the naval where the body bends side to side.

3

Hips

Stand with your heels together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

4

Shoulder

Measure across the back from the tip of one shoulder bone straight to the tip of the opposite shoulder bone.
