Pattukkottai Weaving Legacy

Preserving South Indian Craft, Tailored for the Modern Woman

Rooted in Pattukkottai, Tamil Nadu, Aruvi Fabrics was founded with a quiet devotion to authentic natural textiles. We celebrate the sensory beauty of pure combed cotton and lustrous soft silks.

Every garment in our collection is crafted with breathable comfort, precise shoulder seam cuts, and durable hand-finishing that endures wash after wash.
